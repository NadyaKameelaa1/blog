Creative Process

My creative process involves understanding the project brief, developing visual ideas, refining the illustration, and making revisions when needed. Through my projects, I continue to learn how to balance creativity with project requirements.


From idea to final art

  1. Understand the brief

    Read the request, confirm the goal, style, and deadline.

  2. Develop ideas

    Collect references and sketch a few directions.

  3. Refine

    Clean up line art, then build color and shading.

  4. Revise

    Apply feedback and adjust details when needed.

  5. Deliver

    Export the final illustration in the format requested.
